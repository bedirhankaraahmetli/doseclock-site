DoseClock Support

A medication reminder with no account, no subscription and no data collection.

Questions or problems? Email contact@doseclock.app. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. Never send your medication list or a backup file; we don't need them to help.

Will my reminder make a sound if my iPhone is on silent or in a Focus?

It depends on your iOS version and on one permission.

  • iOS 26 and later, with alarms allowed: a medication set to Alarm uses Apple's alarm system. It sounds even with the silent switch on and in any Focus, and appears on your Lock Screen with a Taken button.
  • Earlier iOS versions, or if alarms are not allowed: reminders are time-sensitive notifications. A Focus such as Sleep will silence them unless you allow time-sensitive notifications for that Focus: Settings → Focus → choose the Focus → Apps → turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. Repeat for every Focus you use.
  • A medication set to Gentle reminder is always an ordinary notification that respects silent mode and Focus.

In DoseClock, tap the bell on the Today screen to open Reminder health. It shows what your iPhone will and won't do, and Test my alarm rings in about ten seconds.

I stopped the alarm but the dose shows as missed

On iOS 26 and later, sliding to stop the alarm only stops the sound. It does not record the dose, because stopping a sound is not the same as taking a medication. Tap Taken on the alarm, or mark the dose on the Today screen.

Do I need to open the app?

iOS lets an app schedule only a limited number of reminders ahead of time. DoseClock schedules as far ahead as it can and tops up whenever you open it. If your reminders are about to run out, DoseClock sends one notification a few days before, saying so. Opening the app then is enough. Reminder health shows the date your reminders currently reach.

What happens when the free trial ends?

When you add your first medication, DoseClock offers the free trial: tap Start free trial and confirm Apple's sheet, which costs nothing. You get 14 days with every feature and as many medications as you like. After that, the reminders you already set keep working, whether you buy or not. The one-time purchase is needed to add or change medications and schedules, add people, and export your history as PDF or CSV. Backup and restore always stay free, so your data is never locked away.

How do I buy, or restore a purchase?

Open Settings and tap Full Access. Buy makes the one-time purchase. Restore purchase brings it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, using your Apple ID. There is no DoseClock account.

Backups: how your data moves without an account

DoseClock has no account and no server, so your information exists in one place: your iPhone. Nothing is synced in the background, and there is no copy anywhere for us to send back to you. Instead, you keep the copy. A backup is a single file, locked with a passphrase you choose, that you save wherever you like: iCloud Drive, the Files app, your computer, or an email to yourself. Saving and restoring a backup is always free.

What a backup contains

Your medications, schedules, dose history, pill counts and refill details, and the people you manage medications for. App settings, such as appearance and the refill reminder time, stay with each iPhone. After a restore, DoseClock sets all your reminders again by itself.

Making a backup

Settings → Your data → Backup. Enter a passphrase, tap Save a backup, and choose where the file goes. A backup holds what you had at the moment you saved it, so save a new one after you change your medications.

Deleting and reinstalling on the same iPhone

Deleting DoseClock deletes its data and cancels its reminders. Save a backup before you delete the app, then restore it after reinstalling. If you only need to free up space, use Offload App instead (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → DoseClock): it removes the app but keeps your data, and everything is still there when you reinstall.

Moving to a new iPhone

Save a backup on your old iPhone, somewhere the new one can reach, such as iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install DoseClock, then go to Settings → Your data → Backup → Restore from a backup, choose the file and enter the passphrase.

An iCloud device backup does not carry DoseClock data to a new phone. This is deliberate: the key that unlocks your data never leaves the iPhone it was made on, so your own backup file is the only way across.

Restoring

Restoring replaces everything on this iPhone with what is in the file. If you already added medications on the new phone, they are replaced. A backup from a newer version of DoseClock needs the app to be updated first.

Your purchase and free trial

These belong to your Apple ID, not to DoseClock and not to the backup file. On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings → Full Access and tap Restore purchase.

If the passphrase is forgotten

Nobody can open the backup, including us. We never see your file or your passphrase, so there is nothing we could reset. Keep the passphrase somewhere safe, apart from your phone.

How do I share my history with my doctor?

Open History, choose the period, tap Export, and pick PDF (to print or send) or CSV (a spreadsheet). The file goes wherever you share it; DoseClock doesn't upload it anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Everything is removed from your iPhone and every reminder is cancelled. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Is this medical advice?

No. DoseClock is a reminder tool. It doesn't recommend doses or check for interactions. Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions.
